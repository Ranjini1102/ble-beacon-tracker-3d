Student Attendance App Workflow

This page emulates the student mobile application used on campus. Students mark attendance each period via their device while inside the classroom.

1
Period Schedule Check (09:00–10:00 AM)

Before 09:00 AM, punch-in is disabled with "Attendance Not Started". From 09:00 to 10:00 AM, the session is active. At 10:00 AM, new punch-ins lock and unpunched students finalize as Absent.

2
Classroom Beacon Proximity Validation

Device continuously monitors BLE packets from the classroom's dedicated ceiling beacon (e.g. BCN-10A). Students cannot mark attendance from other rooms.

3
Face Biometric Verification

Clicking "Punch In" triggers the face verification camera preview. The biometric model verifies the face and records the timestamp.

4
Instant System Synchronization

Punching in immediately updates the 3D classroom scene, the Admin Dashboard, classroom summaries, and the student's status ring.
